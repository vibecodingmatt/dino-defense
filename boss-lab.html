How to use it

  • Boss — every entry in BOSS_DEATHS, including the two that never roam the home screen (the White Pteranodon and the Mosasaurus). Each card previews live.
  • Visitor — the whole cast: a randomised guest, the eight wave-one archetypes, and the three cameos. Click a card, then Send them in to watch that exact person get caught, thrashed, and swallowed on the real menu eat timeline.
  • Muldoon is locked to Blue. They're a set piece in the game and the lab won't let you break the pairing — pick Blue and he unlocks, along with the stalk, his line, and the pounce.
  • Palette — bosses look different on the map than in the home-screen fog. Both are one click apart, because a change that reads on one can disappear in the other.
  • Silhouette is the honest test of a redesign: if the shape doesn't read as solid black, detail won't save it.
